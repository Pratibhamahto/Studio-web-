Frequently
asked questions.

How long does a brand project take?

Most brand identity projects run 6–12 weeks depending on scope and feedback cycles. We'll give you a detailed timeline at kickoff.

Do you work with clients outside Jamshedpur?

Absolutely. The majority of our clients are remote. We work with brands across India and internationally via video calls and our shared project portal.

What do you need from us to get started?

A completed enquiry form is the best first step. Once we review it, we'll schedule a discovery call to dive deeper into your goals.

Do you offer payment in installments?

Yes. We typically split payments into 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, though we're flexible for larger retainer engagements.

Can I see examples of your past work?

Of course. Reach out via the enquiry form and we'll share a tailored case study pack relevant to your industry and project type.

Still have questions? Don't hesitate to reach out directly. We're a small, approachable team and we love a good conversation about design.
